<img src="/media/green.jpg" alt="candy bar" /><br /><br />Green and Black's Bittersweet Dark 70% Chocolate With A Soft Mint Center.  Certainly the most descriptive name for a chocolate bar I've ever seen.  And accurate.  The mint has a very sharp taste, very strong but not overwhelming.  The chocolate is smooth in texture and bitter as described.  I like my dark chocolate around 65%, this is a bit much for me.  The flavors blend very well and if it were a slightly less bitter chocolate this would be a real contender.  The form factor is tall squares about a quarter inch wide and high by about a half inch long.
