Or maybe it's because it sticks around on the page covering up half the content AND other ads (which I'm sure those other advertisers are thrilled about).  It doesn't ever close. I can't even select the 'site fix' topic for this feedback because the ad blocks it. I certainly won't bother trying to read anything else on the Wired.com site as long as that ad is there.<br /><br /></div>Advertising is now part of the internet.  But if advertising makes content viewing impossible, not just inconvenient, that will be the death of ad supported content online.<br />
