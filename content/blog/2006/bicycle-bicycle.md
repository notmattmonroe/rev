Title: Bicycle, bicycle!
Date: 2006-10-16 16:20
Slug: bicycle-bicycle
Category: personal
Status: published

I've had three flat tires in the month or so I've been riding to work.  The barista I see a few times a week who also rides to work looked up while I was telling my co-workers this and said &quot;We really need to talk.&quot;  A guy in line behind me (as well as <a href="http://kev.homelinux.net/">Kevin</a>) opined that there might be burrs on the inside of the rim causing it.  As it turned out the problem was more basic: multiple thorny brambles.  <br /><br />I don't go off the road at all, so I'm guessing somewhere on the sidewalk between my office and the Civic Center Metro Station is where I'm hitting these.  The first one I got while riding a dirt path down to the station rather than taking the long way, which is why I suspect the subsequent two came from the same general area.  I am considering switching to Union Station instead, as it's about equidistant.<br /><br />So I repaired the tube again this weekend and put it back in the tire and got the tire back on.  While I was tweaking the front brakes I could hear the leak on the back tire.  What the hell?  I had to pull the whole tire off again and go inch by inch over the inside of the tire until I found a stray thorn.  Ugh.  This morning I got up and was pleased to see the tires still full of air.  <br /><br />I got on my rain gear (this will take some adjusting to, as it makes me warmer than normal while riding and also makes access to things like wallet and keys more difficult) and headed out.  I got about a block before I realized that my brakes were too loose.  Since that's probably not good in the rain I promptly turned around and got out the tools and tightened them up.  Hassle though the rain gear was, it was nice to get to work completely dry.  I'll need to get a mudguard for the rear tire, and possibly a larger backpack.<br /><br /><br />And probably the only time I'll ever use a Queen quote.
