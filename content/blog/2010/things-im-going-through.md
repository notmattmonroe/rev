Title: The things I'm going through
Date: 2010-04-05 14:04
Slug: things-im-going-through
Category: personal
Status: published

It's a very emotionally turbulent time around my life.  Amongst close friends and family there's active breast cancer, an increasingly desperate job hunt, and divorce happening.  It pains me to see those I love in pain.  If someone you care about is hurting, suffering, depressed, reach out and let them know someone is thinking of them.  It may seem a small thing, but it is so very necessary.<br /><br />Even if you don't realize people are in pain, they are.  Reach out to someone.  You'll feel better.  They'll feel better.
