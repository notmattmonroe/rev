Title: He asked for a 13 but he got a 31
Date: 2010-04-23 13:40

Category: personal
Status: published

Boo likes to declare any pattern she identifies to be received common knowledge.  This morning she told me she recorded an episode of The Wonder Pets (a show she is "way too old for") because it had the Loch Ness Monster.  I knew where this was going.<br /><br />"First it was on Scooby Doo, now it's on Wonder Pets.  You know what they say: once it's on one show, it's on all of them."<br /><br />This mode of explanation is a fine example of what my new tattoo represents.   The <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reification_%28fallacy%29">Gestalt Law of Closure</a>, or the reification fallacy.  The tattoo represents our tendency to seek patterns and to create them where they may not exist, our tendency to see things that aren't there.  It's a tribute to fallibility of our complex brains.<br /><br />One thing I did not think about was the fact that I sit at a computer all day.  My wrist resting on the desk or the wrist support.
