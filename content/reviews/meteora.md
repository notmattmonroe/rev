The much awaited follow up to Hybrid Theory (Reanimation is a remix CD, not an album). The lead single is "Somewhere I Belong" and it's powerful. The album is very personal and deals primarily with relationships in all their phases (though the focus is clearly on the ending of relationships). There are also three fairly impressive instrumentals. Chester's voice has tremendous range, and he can sing soft pieces as easily as screaming ones. Musically strong and consistent, if you liked Hybrid you'll like this.<BR><BR>Hot spots: Breaking The Habit, Somewhere I Belong, Hit The Floor, From The Inside
