I was highly entertained to see that the effort to dislodge the pablumthat is the annual X-Factor Christmas Single from the prestigious<a	href="http://blogs.wsj.com/speakeasy/2009/12/21/rage-against-the-machines-killing-in-the-name-becomes-surprise-christmas-single/">NumberOne Christmas Single</a>position (apparently a very big deal in the UK music industry) resultedin the classic and completely awesome track<a	href="http://www.last.fm/music/Rage+Against+the+Machine/_/Killing+in+the+Name">Killingin the Name Of</a>by<a href="http://www.ratm.com/">Rage Against The Machine</a>coming out on top. And here is the party playlist for my house thisyear. There are quite a number of great modern rock/aggro holiday songsto replace the tired old chestnuts from the past.<br /><br />Every year I fine tune my Christmas collection a bit to eliminate songs that no longer appeal and add in new tracks that I've discovered.  There's quite a few great tracks that aren't on this playlist simply because I wanted a fixed playlist for the morning (presents et al) that all worked well together and didn't involve to many versions of the same song or artist.  There are a handful of multiples but not too many.  While I would have liked to put the entire new Tori Amos album on, for example, the set didn't work well with that much of it on there.  The one thing missing I'd really love to have is Barenaked Ladies and (I think) Sarah McLachlan singing "Baby, It's Cold Outside", but the only version I have is a truncated live version.<br /><br
