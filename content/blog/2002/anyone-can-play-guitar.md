Title: Anyone Can Play Guitar
Date: 2002-12-01 21:17
Slug: anyone-can-play-guitar
Category: personal
Status: published

I've been playing a lot more recently.  Nothing serious, just noodling around, getting the chops back, and so on.  I've taken to learning a new song every day(ish) as a means of expanding my skills.  I don't try to master every nuance of each song, I just pick up the rhythm and perhaps the lead hook, and if it's a particularly cool solo I'll do that as well.    Play along to the MP3 a half dozen times or so and then move on to just jamming on whatever I feel like.  I'm fairly pleased with the results, and in order to make it a little more challenging I just pick something at random from our MP3 server (I limit myself to stuff in the Rock subtree, as classical and hip-hop don't lend themselves as well to this).  This week has been a week of Wallflowers, REM, Default, The Hives, and Semisonic.  Tonight?  Dandy Warhols.
