Not even done listening to the full album through once and <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Postcards-Young-Manic-Street-Preachers/dp/B003OUX2EY/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&s=music&qid=1285165430&sr=8-1">Postcards From A Young Man</a> by <a href="http://www.manicstreetpreachers.com/global/frontpage?cmdr=ip2country/detected">Manic Street Preachers</a> is blowing me away.  There's a bit of choir and orchestra that I am not enamored of on a few tracks, but it strikes a nice balance between the guitar driven path of Send Away the Tigers and the keyboard heavy Lifeblood.  There's a very Queen like aesthetic to late period Manics that I've only really noticed on this album though now that I've recognized it I see the tendency going all the way back to Gold Against the Soul with the obvious exceptions of the Richey albums.  <br /><br />Loving. This. Album.
