Over the weekend, while my wife was off at a diaper party (not the kind that David Vitter would be going to), I danced with my eldest daughter while the babies watched.  And as a credit to her open mindedness and general strangeness, we danced to Frank Zappa.  Bob in Dacron.  Let's Move to Cleveland.  Music for Guitar and Low Budget Orchestra.  It was a lot of fun, and I enjoy exposing her to music she wouldn't normally hear at school or on kids shows or cds.
