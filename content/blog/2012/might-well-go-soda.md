Does anyone remember the above referenced song?  Damn there was some awful metal in the mid 80's.  <p />When I was in college I worked at Wherehouse record stores.  In particular one in Santa Clara right across the street from an Arby's.  This would've been about 1990/91.  Waaaay back in those pre-web days when cell phones were 4 pounds and luggable computers were 25 pounds this Arby's had a touch screen kiosk that let you select your sandwich, drink, sides, etc and pay with your debit card.  In 1991!  <p />Talking to the manager at the time he said that wrong orders (and as a result food waste) plummeted, lines were shorter, customers were happier, profits were up.  6 months later the experiment was pulled by the corporate office and never was heard of again.  <p />Twenty years later the experience of ordering at a fast food restaurant is as idiotic and wasteful as it was then.  Though given the number of people I get behind in the self check out lanes at grocery stores who clearly have no business checking themselves out, perhaps it's for the best.
