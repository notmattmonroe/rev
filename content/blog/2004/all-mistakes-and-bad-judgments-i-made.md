I learn, albeit slowly.  At first, when Narnia would go to sleep for the night (meaning for an expected period of longer than 2 hours), I would see that as an opportunity to get done the many tasks I wasn't able to do while she was awake.  Three months in, I have finally figured out that I will be in much better shape if I go to bed at the same time she does.  She goes to bed at 7pm, I go to bed at 7pm.  She goes to bed at 10pm, I go to bed at 10pm.  That gets me much closer to a normal amount of sleep.<br /><br />Consider this advice for new fathers (Kevin, this means you).<br />
