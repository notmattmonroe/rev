Date: 2010-06-27 14:18
Slug: we-wrote-song-and-it-really-stunk
Category: personal

Pulled out some of my old songwriting books from high school.  How is it that my friends didn't just punch me in the face constantly?  I want to go back in time and hit myself for some of those lyrics.  There's some stuff that I actually really like and will probably record sometime soon, but wow was a lot of it just embarrassingly bad.  Like even 16 year old me should have been mortified at having written them.
