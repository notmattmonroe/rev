So a few scenes from the workshop, as I've not made any obvious progress in a week or so.   First up is the pieces for the dresser carcass, all joints routed and just waiting for me to trim the centerpiece to length (1/4" off each end) and assemble it.  Hopefully this weekend, though the dog complicates that.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/dresser_parts.jpg" alt="Dresser parts" height="400" width="300" /><br /><br />Next up is the parts for the project that's sidetracked the dresser.  The jewelry case that will sit on top of the dresser was declared more important, so I ripped some of the 3/4" ply left over from the dresser to 7" wide and will be making a two foot tall by three foot wide by seven inch deep jewelry case.  The drawers are going to be a pain.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/jewelry_case.jpg" alt="Jewelry Case parts" height="400" width="300" /><br /><br />And last but not least much of my scrap wood.  I need to sort through and toss the crap and better organize what I keep, but that requires time.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/scrap.jpg" alt="scrap parts" height="300" width="400" /><br /><br />I do try to have as little waste as possible on projects, and try to reuse whenever I can, but inevitably end up not having quite the right dimensions on hand and having to go buy more wood. For the Dresser/Vanity/Jewelry Case trio (and apparently soon to follow a shoe storage system) I've managed to plan my cuts so that I can build the majority of it out of two sheets of 4'x8' 3/4" ply (not including the 13 dresser drawers and 15 jewelry drawers, which will all come out of a 4x8 sheet of 1/2" ply for the dresser and an 8' 1x10 for the jewelry drawers) with almost no leftovers at all.
