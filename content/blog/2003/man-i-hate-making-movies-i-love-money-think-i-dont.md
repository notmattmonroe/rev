If there were more movies like <a href="http://us.imdb.com/Title?0280707">Gosford Park</a> and fewer like <a href="http://us.imdb.com/Title?0242423">Dude, where's my car?</a>.  I'd probably see a lot more movies.  Seriously.  A solid, complex plot.  Believable characters.  Excellent acting. Lush cinematography.  Incredible attention to period detail.  This is how movies should be.  There's nothing wrong with lowbrow comedy, I love comedies.  But they have to be funny <a href="http://casachristy.bloghorn.com/default.aspx?id=563">clever</a>, not funny stupid.
