Six musical questions from <a href="http://scienceblogs.com/principles/2009/04/six_questions_an_attempt_at_a.php">Chad Orzel</a>.<br /><br />Who Need the Peace Corps? - Frank Zappa: Zappa was famously unimpressed with the hippy scene and mocks them in this second single from the brilliant We're Only In It For The Money.<br /><br />Where Did Your Heart Go? - Was (Not Was).  Some of the best R & B workouts of the 80's came from these guys.  The WHAM! version is a poor cover of the original.<br /><br />What I Say? - Miles Davis.  Sublime, this version is fromt The Cellar Door Sessions.<br /><br />How U Feelin? - Peeping Tom.  One of Mike Patton's current bands.  He described this album as his take on what he thinks popular music should sound like.  <br /><br />Why Do You Bother? - Faith No More.  From the Chuck Mosley era, he was really a strong lyricist but lacked the consistency of Patton both in writing and performance.<br /><br />When Will We B Paid? - Prince.  Good gospel influenced lament on the history of African Americans.<br /><br />The artist distribution is no surprise, generating a random playlist from my collection is highly likely to include Prince, Zappa, and some variation of Mike Patton as between the three they comprise 30% of my library.
