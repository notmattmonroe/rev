Thoughts thoughts thoughts.  Today is going to be busy, so I'm kicking this out fast while I have a spare :30.<p>Gartner says Linux servers ^ 100% next year.  Note the growth will come at the expense of RISC based boxes rather than MS boxes.  My concern is that I'm not entirely convinced that x86 is the right choice for high end servers.  Workstations/desktops are fine on Intelish systems but high end really needs the architectural advantages that x86 simply doesn't have.  I think this will bite Linux in the ass as it's bitten MS in the ass.  People blame the OS for the shortcomings of the architecture.  For all the bitching about blue screens, I never heard the level of complaints from people using NT on Alphas as I did from people using NT on Intel.  Consider that for a moment.<p>W3C rejects RAND.
