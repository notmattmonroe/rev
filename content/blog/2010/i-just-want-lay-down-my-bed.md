Been pushing extra hard for a few weeks now, letting sleep take a back seat.  We are going to a Mixmas party this weekend, everyone brings a mix cd or two to trade with people and there are prizes for things like 'best theme' etc etc.  I curated several different cds and have had them all ready for a few days.  In addition Jaden and I are collaborating on a fairly ambitious mix cd that we think may get a special prize.  And because I can't leave well enough alone I'm making another last minute mix CD, an Introduction to the Manic Street Preachers.<br /><br/>It gives a full spectrum overview of their career and development as artists.  I considered including some of the solo project work, but decided to go purist and only include tracks released under the group name.  From early post punk to current elegaic rock with detours into the dual Richey-centric horrorshows Holy Bible and Journal for Plague Lovers.
