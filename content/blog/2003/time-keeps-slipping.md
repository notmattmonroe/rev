There is something seriously wrong with this world.<p>Which brings me to "Are you Hot?" or whatever the hell the latest "reality" show from ABC is called.  <a href="http://www.maemidwest.com">Mae</a> turned to me yesterday when that commercial came on and said "Maybe it wouldn't be such a bad thing if the world was blown to hell."
