Title: Gotta break away from this delusion
Date: 2010-02-18 19:37
Slug: gotta-break-away-delusion
Category: personal
Status: published

I have in the past been very critical of Chris Mooney, particularly on the issue of Science Communication.  I haven't read Unscientific America, though I suspect I would overall like it in spite of the controversy surrounding it.  When it was announced that he would be one of the replacements for D.J. Groethe on the Point of Inquiry podcast I was concerned that he wouldn't bring the sort of intellectual heft and personable tough questioning that Groethe did but would rather focus on castigating scientists for 'doing it wrong'.<br/><br/>Having now listened to the first episode with Mooney, an interview with Dr. Paul Offit, I can say I was impressed.  Mooney isn't as polished a speaker as Groethe, but he is a good interviewer and asked excellent questions.  I did miss Groethe's tremendous ability to play devil's advocate or to disagree vehemently yet politely with his guests, but Groethe is a rare interviewer unmatched by any of his contemporaries in my view.  I look forward to Mooney's future episodes of Point of Inquiry, and wish him well.<br /><br />And I've still got Groethe on For Good Reason.  The problem with this new situation where POI continues with different hosts and Groethe is at FGR is that keeping up with my podcasts becomes more difficult.
