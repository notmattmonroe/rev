The most dangerous band in hip hop. Like most of the intellectual hip hop artists, The Roots talk to the problems facing young black people in America from a standpoint that there are answers other than dealing and stealing. On tracks like Everybody Is a Star they take a big risk and talk about sexism within the black community, which has been a very touchy subject. Taking their name from the sociology work of Malcolm Gladwell and his book on the influence of small numbers of people in creating change.
