This is a quickie project.  It took longer for two coats of paint to dry than to cut and glue it.  I mostly skipped the design phase on this one.  I piece of scrap 1x10, some spare hardboard, and some scrap 3/4" ply and two brackets plus about 45 minutes in the garage built this little bedside drawer.  9 1/2" wide, 9 " deep, and 5" tall.  We bought a new bed a few months back and it pushed our already tight setup along the wall over into the doorway.  This allows us to get in and out the door without scraping our legs on the nightstand.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/float2.jpg" alt="drawer open" height="400" width="300" /><br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/float1.jpg" alt="drawer closed" height="400" width="300" />I may pull it down at some point to pretty up the paint job, but other than that and it being a little tight of  a space for everything we have on it (limited by the wood I had on hand) I'm fairly happy with it.
