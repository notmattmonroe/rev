The Pew Research Center has a nice little <a href="http://pewresearch.org/newsiq/">News IQ quiz</a>.  I thought it was way too easy, but the scary thing is I am sure a majority of Americans would get less than 60% on it.  I missed a single question (on the number of troop casualties in Iraq) and it dropped me to the 85th percentile. UPDATE: CNN has a <a href="http://www.cnn.com/2008/LIVING/01/23/news.quiz/index.html">weekly news quiz</a>, but in at least one of the questions the accompanying photo includes the answer.  Nicely done.  I missed the sports question, of course.<br /><br />Interesting news out of China about a <a href="http://www.physorg.com/news120292812.html">100,000 year old human skill</a>.   What I found most interesting was the quoting of an academic that contradicts the assertions of the newspaper.  I could tell that article did not come from an American source.  It would be a rare and wonderful thing for an American paper or wire service to publish an academic  that was disagreeing with a media outlet.  Sure enough it was AFP, Agence France-Presse.<br /><br />Something else possibly older than previously thought:  <a href="http://scienceblogs.com/afarensis/2008/01/23/the_platypus_may_be_older_than/">The Platypus</a>.
