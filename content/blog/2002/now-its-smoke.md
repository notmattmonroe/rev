Title: Now its smoke
Date: 2002-12-23 04:51
Slug: now-its-smoke
Category: personal
Status: published

I am beginning to think I need a special wardrobe just for going to clubs.  Nothing particularly fancy, a pair of jeans, a pair of slacks, some nice shirts, a jacket, shoes, socks, underwear.  Pretty much everything.   That way when I get home from a night out clubbing, I could leave them all in the garage so my house didn't reek of cigarettes.  Ah well.  The price one must pay to go out and have fun is smelling like stale smoke.
