Got off the Metro and was confronted with a line of teenagers in all white sweatsuits with letters on them spelling out &quot;S H O U T I T O U T&quot;.  They were exhorting the commuters exiting the train to &quot;C'mon, shout it out with us!&quot;.  Very enthusiastic kids. <br /><br /> I looked around for the TV cameras as this struck me as the sort of thing you'd see in a commercial but never in real life.  I saw no cameras and neglected to take a picture myself because I was more interested in getting out of the cold wind.
