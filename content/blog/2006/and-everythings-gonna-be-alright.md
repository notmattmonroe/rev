Many times in my IT career I've heard people relate their experience with CA.  It generally revolved around the battle to get away from their product and move to a competing product that they ended up being much happier with.  More often than not, the competitor would then be bought by CA and the superior product desupported and left to die while current users of the product were pushed to move to CA's own product instead.  <br /><br />Back when we were evaluating scheduling tools, CA7 was one of the competitors, but the combination of features and the barely restrained hostility of those people with CA experience caused the agency to choose Cybermation.  And so now several million dollars and almost two years later, we are ready to implement it.  And last week CA announced they are buying Cybermation.<br /><br />The best part, to me, is that one of the guys with CA experience said &quot;It doesn't matter, whatever we buy will be bought and killed by CA anyway.&quot;
