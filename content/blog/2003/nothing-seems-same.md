Many of the things that seemed so important in life Before Conception lost much of their weight rather quickly.  I realize this is not an amazing insight, that it is in fact one that the vast majority of parents in the world have realized.  It's just that I've now joined that subset of humanity and am having a personal realization of it.  <p>I hope to accomplish some things today, as I really didn't yesterday, but if I don't then oh well.  It's not that big a deal anymore.  Of course, it all has to get done eventually, but if it's a day or so later then that's OK.  Suddenly I've become Stuart.  And thats... OK.
