n a shock to the music industry, Radiohead have announced that <a href="http://www.inrainbows.com/Store/index3.html">fans will be able to decide how much to pay </a>for their new album if they buy it as a download.  </p><p>This is big for several reasons.  First, Radiohead have been highly resistant to digital delivery.  Second, they are doing this 100% on their own with no record label. Third, there had only been vague rumours of an album coming out this year for a week or so and they went from zero to &quot;It's out next week&quot; with no leaks and no speculation.  Digital release two months before physical ship is certainly the way the industry is going, but for top name artists who have a fanatical following and remain a top touring attraction it's nice to see.</p><p>The box set interests me (one extra CD, two 12&quot; vinyl disks, and an extra disk worth of content as download) but I no longer have a record player so half of it would be of no use to me :(  I am going to assume that they'll eventually do what Barenaked Ladies did and release all the songs associated with the album as a digital collection.
