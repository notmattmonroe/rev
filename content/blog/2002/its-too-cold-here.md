Title: It's too cold here
Date: 2002-12-04 14:07
Slug: its-too-cold-here
Category: personal
Status: published

I love the first snow of the year.  I hate the fact that most people seem to get even more stupid when the first snow hits.  My commute is normally about 40 minutes.  Today, it was 2 and a half hours.  I saw 11 accidents and at least two dozen near misses, so I'm still glad I took the bus.  I did get a lot of reading done, however, and now I'm finally at work.  I'll have a compressed day but I can get it all done.  Ugh.<br /><br />It's not even two inches of snow, people.
