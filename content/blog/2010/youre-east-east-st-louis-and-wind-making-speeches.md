There is never enough time.  The schedule is so tightly packed and there is always so much to do that there never seems to be the critical time for processing.  My mind needs time to resolve the dissonances, to comprehend what has happened and prepare for what is to happen.  I need to find a way to get that capacity.  <br /><br />This year has been more full of chaos and high-intensity events so far and promises to only continue such and I've not had time to ruminate on the events of February or March let alone keep up with what's happening today or our pending family reunion.  I've made notes to remind myself of my state of mind over time, I really need to go back and look over those.<br /><br />I could use about a month of just staring at the ceiling thinking.
