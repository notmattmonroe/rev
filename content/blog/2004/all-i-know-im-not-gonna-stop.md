Title: All I know is I'm not gonna stop
Date: 2004-03-11 15:33
Slug: all-i-know-im-not-gonna-stop
Category: personal
Status: published

Resource contention is a fact of life.  Be it vying for meetings with critical persons or the unending stream of tasks around the house.  Sometimes you just need to stop giving up resources and recover.  Everyone has their own way.  Meditation, excercise, reading, any sort of space that helps you to recuperate.  I've always found that solace in playing guitar, but the opportunities to do so are quite limited of late.<br /><br />I had lunch with <a href="http://www.angerisagift.net/">anger</a> yesterday, and we talked at great length about fatherhood.  It's nice to really be able to talk about what I'm experiencing with someone who not only understands, but isn't bored with it.   He has that same passion for his kids as I do, and they're at the similar stages of development, so I feel like he is in the same mental place I am.  We talked about how at the end of a crappy day at work, the best therapy of all is to just sit down and hang out with your kid(s).  <br /><br />And if you're lucky you can play guitar for them as well.  <br />
