While it was solidly established years ago that the Wakefield research on linking vaccines and autism was just plain wrong, it's now come out that he was paid hundreds of thousands of dollars to create that research by lawyers hoping to strike it rich suing the vaccine manufacturers.  The only thing more disgusting than the perversion of scientific research is the blase manipulation of the hopes and fears the millions of families with autistic children.  Amazing what greed will do to people.
