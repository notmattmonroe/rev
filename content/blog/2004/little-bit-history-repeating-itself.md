I meant to write add this note last night, but I was pretty groggy...<br /><br />I find it interesting that an artist with no record contract, no label,no distributor, and no coverage on the &quot;music&quot; channels is able tolaunch an arena tour that is selling out.  And doing it all on his ownterms.  That's how music should be.  Artists making the real money fromtheir live performances and merch sales.  <br /><br />Keep in mind that musicianspretty much only made money from their live performances from thedawn of time up until the middle of the last century.  The recordindustry is an aberration of history and is in the process of being obviated.  As digital delivery takes the place of physical delivery, recorded music will serve largely as promotional material for the concerts.  <br /><br />I think that's a good thing.    <br />
