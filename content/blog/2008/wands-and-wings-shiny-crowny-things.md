My eldest daughter is quite a character.  She sings and dances and concocts elaborate stories that her dolls then act out.  So a fairly typical 4 year old girl. She stopped,  looked innocently up at me,  and said "<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fairly_OddParents">Uhm, internet?</a>"
