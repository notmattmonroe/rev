The Roots are the best band you never heard of. Jay Z chose them to back him for his Unplugged appearance, and they overshadowed the master. More than a hip-hop band (there's such a thing? Yes, they actually play instruments), they delve into punk, rock, soul, and techno throughout the course of Phrenology. In a genre full of clever lyricists, the Roots have an impressive intellect to back up their vocabulary.<br /><br />Hot Spots: Sacrifice (feat. Nelly Furtado), WAOK Roll Call, Thought At Work
