And it begins.  The biggest furniture project yet, a dresser.  I'm confident mechanically about this one but a little trepidatious about the drawer fronts, but relatively simple to redo those if I'm unhappy with them.  It's going to be 64" wide, 20" deep, and 31" high, not including the Jewelry Storage System.  <br /><br />3/4" baltic birch.  Yes, it's going to be *heavy*.  I am thinking of putting locking wheels under the base.  Due to the nature of the wood, width cutting the major components drained both 18V batteries before I finished.  I need to get a plug-in circular saw!  Fortunately there was only about 10" left to cut on the last piece so I just used a handsaw.  Interestingly it was just as quick going with the handsaw.  If I could handcut as straight and clean as I can with a circular I'd do it for everything.  Practice practice practice, of course.<br /><br />My goal is to have the carcass complete by the end of the weekend (height cut all pieces, route all dados and rabbets, assemble, and it seems a fairly acheivable goal given how hard I rocked the width cutting last night.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/dresser1.jpg" alt="plywood sheets that will one day be a dresser" width="400" height="300" />
