Sometimes the best way to understand a concept is through visualization.  <a href="http://www.codinghorror.com/blog/archives/000976.html">Coding Horror has a great explanation of SQL joins</a> that made crystal clear a concept I'd always struggled with.<br /><br />If you want to see the dangers of recursion (or iteration abstraction) do this:<br /><br />Fire up a VNC server on your machine.  <br />Fire up your VNC client.  <br />Connect to your machine.  <br />Watch the windows cascade and take your system down.  <br /><br />It helps to have another machine handy with which to shell in and kill the process.  This is something I did this morning in order to test that my private vpn was set up properly without thinking about the whole recursion issue.  <br /><br /><br />
