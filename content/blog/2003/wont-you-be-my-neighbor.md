It's <a href="http://www.cnn.com/2003/SHOWBIZ/TV/02/27/rogers.obit/index.html">not a beautiful day</a> in the neighborhood.<p>Back in high school, I had just about the coolest teacher ever for English.  Fred Handsfield.  He was a big fan of Mr Rogers.  He had an autographed picture on his desk.  The class that was in session when the show came on got to watch part of it.  Aside from that he was a brilliant teacher, able to motivate even recalcitrant kids such as myself.
