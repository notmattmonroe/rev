Another entry for the STLBloggers <a href="http://stlbloggers.com/689c#Comments">poetry contest</a>, something I wrote almost precisely 10 years ago today:<br /><br />I work hard all day, trying to make ends meet <br />I suppress my personality<br />I do what I'm told, and try to never think<br />sometimes I forget that I'm free<br />I don't agree with all that I'm told <br />I just do my job<br /> <br />Maybe right now you can't see <br />you tell me that you hate me<br />You say you can't respect me <br />but someday you'll be just like me<br /> <br />My train of thought derails<br />life's a liquid poisonous dream<br />memory starts to fail<br />floating, stopped, caught in midstream<br /> <br />As I've lived my life, I've seen lots of shit<br />and I've learned to deal with it<br />I've learned the importance of situational ethics <br />and I find I've no regrets<br />
