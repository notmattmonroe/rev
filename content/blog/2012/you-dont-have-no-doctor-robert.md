Title:  You don't have no doctor Robert
Date: 2012-11-30 22:15
Slug: You-dont-have-no-doctor-Robert
Category: personal
Status: published

I do a fair amount of audio editing, between chopping up audio files of live shows I'm pulling off of old cassettes (yes, cassettes) or putting together intros for the stage shows I work on or just recording songs I've written.  For about a decade I've been using Amadeus as my track editor and it's done a respectable job in spite of it's quirks and crashes.  I'm still using the long out of support version 2, so I can't complain much.<p />Every once in a while I'll demo the currently popular tools to see if I want to move.  I've been tempted by Sound Studio several times but I'm a cheap bastard so I haven't done it.  Recently <A href="http://www.rogueamoeba.com/fission/">Fission</a> was on sale and I've been really pleased with their Audio HiJack Pro and SoundSource so I decided to buy it after demoing it again.  <p />I didn't think about the fact that this is a pure track editor, you can't create new files in Fission, but that's not really a big deal as I can do that fine using quicktime or garage band.  I've found it to be quick, absurdly easy to use, and I have a personal affinity for its' "do one thing and do it well" philosophy.<p />The functionality is limited to cutting, trimming, pasting, fade in/out, normalize levels, crop, and insert silence.  This makes it real quick and easy to get done what you need and get out.  Bring the edited file into your garage band or wherever and throw effects at it etc.  But what motivated me to write this is the smart split feature.  I hadn't used it at all yet, but I was wanting to pull some songs from a couple of podcasts I have (mostly <a href="http://coverville.com">Coverville</a>) and this was the first time I've used Fission for that.  Smart Split just damn works.  It broke out the entire podcast into the spoken segments and the songs and saved me craploads of time and effort.
