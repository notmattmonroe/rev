Every time there is a conference the issue of "why aren't there more women (or minorities)?" arises.<br /><br />And along comes The Amaz!ng Meeting who dispense with the bloviating and just DO IT.  Check out this damned lineup: <a href="http://www.amazingmeeting.com/speakers">Amaz!ng Meeting Speakers</a>. Don't have endless discussions about outreach and inclusiveness, just DO IT.  <br /><br />I would love to go this year!
