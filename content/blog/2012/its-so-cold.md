Title: It's so cold
Date: 2012-01-25 18:24
Slug: its-so-cold
Category: personal
Status: published

The minute you start relying on a conspiracy for an explanation of your ideology you lose all credibility.  It is in fact one of my several simple test to determine if someone should be taken seriously.  Yes, there are conspiracies.  The larger and more complicated the theory the less likely it is to be true, and people who base their ideologies on conspiracy theories tend to have the most massive and convoluted theories of all.  A conspiracy that scales beyond a few dozen people for longer than a few years demands a tremendous amount of evidence.  Conspiracies that involve thousands of people over decades?  Technically possible, but with a probability that is near enough to zero as to be indistinguishable.
