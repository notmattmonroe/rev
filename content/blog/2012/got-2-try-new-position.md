The new house is an opportunity to fix things that weren't working out.  One of the first changes I've made is my main worktop.  The table saw and router table were side by side giving me very little outfeed support and making work with the circular or jig saw annoying and difficult.  So as I had to take the workstation apart to get it into the basement anyway* I decided to alter the orientation of the table saw.  It was turned 90 degrees and now the router table acts as outfeed support for the table saw.  In addition there is now a gap of about 6 inches between the two  surfaces, so I can do work with hand power tools over the gap and have plenty of support for the work pieces and the cutoff.
