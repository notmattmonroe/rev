Title: It's a matter of opinion as a matter of fact
Date: 2006-03-08 11:58
Slug: its-matter-opinion-matter-fact
Category: personal
Status: published

've been thinking about music a lot lately as I am wont to do. Penn was talking the other day about how &quot;nobody likes ALL music&quot; which ties in nicely to my comments about the subjectivity of musical taste. I think it's important that not only can you say &quot;band x sucks&quot;, but I think you SHOULD. Make people think about their taste in music. <br /><br />As with any other subject I think it's important to challenge. You may end up reaffirming your opinions on a topic, you may alter them slightly, or you may change them completely. Regardless of the specific result you will learn more about yourself. Which is always good.<br /><br />&quot;Don't just question authority, question EVERYTHING! Even me!&quot; - Jello Biafra<br /><br />And as I've been remiss on reviewing music for the past year I have just a few quick observations. I hope this will trigger a steady flow of book and music reviews, more than a dozen of which I have already half written.<br /><br />Black Rebel Motorcycle Club is way more tame than you'd expect. Some nice guitar work, but largely forgettable.<br /><br />Brightwood is emo crap<br /><br />Broken Family Band is too. What the fuck is &quot;alt country&quot;<br /><br />Fallout Boy is not as bad as Brightwood, but it's depressing to hear music this boring and realize they're incredibly popular<br /><br />Jenny Lewis and the Watson Twins -  I think Lewis has a good voice, but her style is very affected.  Like many indie darlings, the &quot;art for art's sake&quot; pose seems like just that: a pose.<br /><br />The Go! Team - mediocre spirit squad rejects<br /><br />Sons and Daughters - interesting modernization of folk music, worth listening to.<br /><br />Stellastar - They would like to be the Cure. They're not bad, but they're not the Cure.<br /><br /><br />
