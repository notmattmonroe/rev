OK, so LAN Support has been replacing CRT monitors with LCDs. They come around with a cart to your cube, your name is on the list so they swap out your CRT for an LCD. Our builder/deployer swapped his CRT for an LCD that was left behind when someone quit so he already had a late model one. But his name was on the list, so they swapped out his 20" LCD for a 20" LCD.  The list is all important.<br /><br />Then while he was at lunch our software architect came over complaining that his new laptop is 16x9 but his monitor is 4x3 so it's ugly and screwed up. I was so so so hoping that the LCD they gave the builder was a 16x9 so we could swap it for the architects and the builder would come back from lunch to basically the same LCD that they'd just replaced this morning.<br /><br />Twas not to be, but that would have made this a truly fantastic day.
