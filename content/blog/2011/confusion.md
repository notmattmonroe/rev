Category: personal
Status: published

I was reading something somewhere online and it referred to the many top hits by the Alan Parsons Project.  This struck me as decidedly odd.  The only song of theirs I could think of was Time (is flowing like a river).  I tried to imagine how a fairly avante-garde jazz fusion guitarist would end up with a bunch of top 40 hits.  I suppose versatility must be the answer and moved on.  Then the other night it suddenly hit me:  Alan Parsons is not Alan Holdsworth.  Suddenly the last 30 years make slightly more sense than they did before that revelation.<br /><br />I also frequently confuse Kim Deal and Kim Thayil.
