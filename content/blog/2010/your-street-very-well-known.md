Two quick shots from the weekend.  I came to the conclusion that I hated the drawers I built for the jewelry case.  Too bulky, the material is too thick (1" stock), and after sanding all the joints smooth there was about 1/8 inch of play on the sides when in the case and that's simply not acceptable.  So the kids and I went to home depot to look over the paucity of wood options and pick the least worst option, 1/2" sanded ply.  I expect to power through the drawers before the end of the work.
