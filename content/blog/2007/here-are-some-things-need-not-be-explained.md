Some light reading for a Friday. <a href="http://sandwalk.blogspot.com/2007/12/accelerated-human-evolution.html">Larry Moran</a> has an awesome entry on the continuing evolution of our species*. In part he was motivated by the <a href="http://www.nytimes.com/2007/12/11/science/11gene.html?_r=2&ref=science&oref=slogin&oref=slogin">Selection Spurred Recent Evolution</a> article in the NYT on Tuesday. And <a href="http://scienceblogs.com/evolgen/2007/12/recent_accelerated_adaptive_ev.php">Evolgen</a> provides links to a bunch of commentary on the study, including some by one of the co-authors.<br /><br />I read the actual study and found it fascinating, I highly recommend it.  More importantly, the assorted commentaries on it reflect a *real* controversy in evolution, not the faux-troversy of &quot;intelligent design -vs- evolution&quot;.  This is actual scientists debating a point of disagreement on the process of evolution, and I agree that this sort of controversy should be taught in schools.<br /><br /><br />*this assumes that if you are reading this and comprehending it chances are you are of the species Homo Sapiens Sapiens<br /><br /><br />
