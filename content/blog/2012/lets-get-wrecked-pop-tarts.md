I was cooking* lunch recently and noticed this bit of instruction: "Cooking the pizza to one side in the microwave may help it to bake more evenly".   Really?  I'm curious as to why this is.  Any of you science-y types who have any theories on this I'd be curious to hear them.*On the box of Pop-Tarts it says "1. Remove pastry from pouch. 2. Well at that point you're cooking.  Anything with more than two steps is cooking.  - Paula Poundstone.
