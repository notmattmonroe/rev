Title: The elements in harmony
Date: 2012-04-16 15:51
Slug: elements-harmony
Category: personal
Status: published

If you are making the claim that you know biology better than all biologists in the world and that their understanding of biology is wrong, you are by extension asserting that all chemistry and physics are wrong as well.  Not just incomplete, as we know they are, but actually claiming that what we do know is incorrect and doesn't work the way we believe it does.  To declare all of science to be wrong requires an extremely convincing set of evidence.  "Because I said so" fails as evidence.  The scientific disciplines inform, rely on, bolster, and test one another.    What we know about biological systems requires that chemical reactions and physics work the way that we understand them to.  Which we demonstrate that they do tens of thousands of times every day in labs across the world.
