The new metal genre is overflowing with bad bands with worse lyrics and cliche riffs. Godsmack is one of the rare exceptions. While full of the seriousness and sullenness you would expect, Faceless manages to have that extra edge that helps them stand out from the crowd.<BR><BR>Hot spots: Faceless, Make Me Believe, Releasing the Demons, Serenity.
