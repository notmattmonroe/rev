Title: Just eat it
Date: 2003-04-06 20:38
Slug: just-eat-it
Category: personal
Status: published

When I was a kid, I ate a lot of strange things.  I would make sandwiches out of everything in the fridge, trying to imitate the Dagwood approach.  These were generally inedible.  I also was known for always having food with me, generally in a little sandwich baggie.  Usually Oreo's.  This afternoon, I remembered my favorite dessert.   I would take a bowl of ice cream (any flavor), break up several cookies into it, pour in some milk, and stir it up into a soupy mess.  As usual, everyone thought it was gross and that I was nuts.  They now sell these for $3.50 each at Dairy Queen and call them Blizzards.  I always knew I was ahead of my time, I just didn't realize it was in the field of desserts.
