92 degrees is harsh. I may need to try and figure out a time to go to the gym other than noon. Then again, it certainly gets my heart rate up. <br /><br />On to more important. What the hell is it about people that walk slow that they need to get in your way as much as possible? This is particularly a problem getting on/off MetroLink, but even in the various office buildings I frequent I end up trapped behind someone in no particular hurry. <br /><br />And these are not elderly people. I understand their limitations. These are not people hauling children with them, I understand that as well. I don't even mean groups of people that end up walking slowly. While that is annoying, I can generally make my way through them and they'll step aside if I say &quot;pardon me&quot;.<br /><br />I'm talking about able bodied, mostly healthy looking people in their 20's, 30's, and 40's. Whether they are dressed trailer or Ladue doesn't seem to matter. People who are not only slow, but walk in the middle of the hallway or walkway, and *don't move out of your way when you say 'excuse me'*. That is what drives me nuts.<br /><br />Me getting to my desk 1-2 minutes late is no big deal. Me missing my connecting bus and being 40 minutes late because some idiot couldn't be bothered to move over 14 inches is a big deal. To me. I realize they don't care. <br /><br />I've stopped being nice about it. Someone is in my way, I don't even pause. I'll barrel through. I say 'pardon me', and I might even try not to bump into them. Maybe. But I'm not so overloaded with free time that I'm interested in wasting any of it because some idiot is dawdling. <br /><br />Totally unrelated: Heard the Paris Hilton song.
