The NPR scripts are for scheduled recording of particular NPR shows sothey can be moved to an iPod or some lesser MP3 device. I use AudioHijack Pro from Rogue Amoeba with the schedule helper installed andhighly recommend it. There are other similar tools out there, and I'mfairly sure you could even do this with the lite version of AudioHijack.<br><br>In Audio Hijack:<br><br><ol><li>&nbsp;create a new Session</li><li>&nbsp;select the audio source of "application"</li><li>&nbsp;select "Safari"</li><li>&nbsp;check the "Open URL/File/Applescript"</li><li>&nbsp;browse to the saved script on your machine</li><li>&nbsp;set the schedule to record at the appropriatedays/times. </li></ol><br>Current scripts:<br>Wait Wait... Don't Tell Me - Run Sunday late afternoon<br><br>Whad'ya Know? - Run Saturday late afternoon
