Michael Franti and crew are back and better than ever. Sweet melodies, complex rhymes, and agitprop to sooth the soul<br /><br />The man behind Disposable Heroes of Hiphoprisy (the best name ever) leads a solid group highlighted by dozens of guests (including Zap Mama, Woody Harrelson, and Radio(Active)). Jazzy swingin funk regaling the crimes of the government, the inequalities of society, and interspersed with radio clips, the album is a resurgence of the activism of the music of the 60's. This is some deep thoughts and some deep grooves to keep your body and your mind active.
