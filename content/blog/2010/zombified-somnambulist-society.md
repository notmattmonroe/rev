Awesome post from Information Is Beautiful about the <a href="http://www.informationisbeautiful.net/play/snake-oil-supplements/">relative effectiveness of popular health supplements</a>.  Supplements are one of the more complicated things, OK really the *only* complicated thing, about 'Alternative' Medicine.  Chiropractic is simple: outside of low level physical therapy and the placebo effect it's a crock.  Acupuncture?  Same thing.  Reiki?  Again.  Supplements are different.  They have to be taken on a case by case basis.  Real medicines have their foundation in the plant world.  Most are derived from plants in some way or another.  The catch is that picking a couple leaves of something versus getting a prescription or OTC medication is consistency of does.  Those leaves will have wildly different amounts of the active ingredient so you don't know how much you're actually getting.<br /<br />As Ted Dziuba notes: Whole Foods has the biggest snake oil section of any grocery store I've seen yet.
