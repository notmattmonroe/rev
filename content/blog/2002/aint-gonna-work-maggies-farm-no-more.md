Got feedback on the screen design for the new portal.  It's all great, except the image of a farmer on a tractor, there will be a violently negative reaction if we submit that to the users.  Apparently there was a fairly acrimonious seperation of responsibility a few years back within the USDA, with farm services and rural utilities being cleaved one from the other.  To dare to include the (aesthetically pleasing, contemplative, and dare I say patriotic) image of a farmer at work in the early morning would offend the sensibilities of the staff.  No problem, I'm not married to any of my design ideas.  You don't like it?  I'll replace it with a shot of a power plant or something else.  What a strange thing to get upset about.  I didn't realize the totally non-functional portion would be the Persian flaw ;)
