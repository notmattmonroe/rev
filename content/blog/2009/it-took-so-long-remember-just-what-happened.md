Title: It took so long to remember just what happened
Date: 2009-02-13 14:31
Slug: it-took-so-long-remember-just-what-happened
Category: personal
Status: published

In what is an entirely predictable result of the privatization of prisons, two judges <a href="http://www.nytimes.com/2009/02/13/us/13judge.html?_r=1&hp">pled guilty to taking kickbacks to send kids to prison</a>.  I very seriously doubt this is an isolated case and I would not be the slightest bit surprised to find that this was happening with adult defendants as well.  The other entirely predictable result of such privatization: prison companies lobby for harsher laws and penalties to ensure their profits, often writing the sample legislation themselves.  The justice system should be driven by justice, not profits.  Once the profit motive enters the picture corruption is guaranteed and justice is the least priority.
