Title: Train keeps a rolling
Date: 2006-08-29 17:22
Slug: train-keeps-rolling
Category: personal
Status: published

The Metro Extension is very nice.  The new stations look great and are well located.  I rode my bike to the station and my total time door to door was about the same as the old bus/train combo I have done for the past several years.  But more convenient.  The problem with the buses is that outside of commute hours it can take more than twice as long to get to work or get home.  If I leave work at 3.15 I get home at the same time as if I left at 4.  And the entirety of that extra time is spent waiting at the transfer station.  <br /><br />With the train/bike combo there is no transfer, so I have to wait a maximum of ten minutes for my train at the station and that's it.  So if I'm going to go in late or leave early it's feasible now.  This is wonderful.<br /><br />Not so wonderful is that I didn't realize there are a number of hills between house and station.  And I haven't ridden in 5 years.  I will be sore for the first few weeks, I suspect.<br />
