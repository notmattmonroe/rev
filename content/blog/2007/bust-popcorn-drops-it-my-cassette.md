As she traipsed through the dining room she announced &quot;I'm not carrying anything!&quot;  So I glanced over and saw that she had a whole bowl full halloween popcorn* from <a href="http://www.poptionspopcorn.com/">Poptions</a>.<br /><br />&quot;Ah, no.  Put that on the counter in the kitchen&quot; says I.  She at least handles getting caught gracefully:  She blithely coos &quot;OK&quot; in a tone that said  that was her plan the whole time and having done so continues on to the tv room as if she had any interest in doing anything in there other than eat an entire bowl of halloween popcorn.<br /><br />*popcorn covered with caramel, dark chocolate, white chocolate, and mixed in with candy corn.
