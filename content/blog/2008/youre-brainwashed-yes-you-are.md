Today was another evangelist proselytizing to a gullible audience.  In this case a guy who identified himself as "a .Net guy" spent the better part of a half hour explaining to another guy and a few onlookers why the iPod is the worst MP3 player on the market and Apple is evil and you're locked in to their platform forever.  Among the false claims he made, most of them repeatedly:<ul><li>You can only play music from the iTunes Music Store on the iPod.<li>You can only play that music on one device, so if you authorize it to play on the iPod you can't play it on your computer.<li>You can't stream the music to your TV or stereo at all unless you buy more Apple hardware.<li>If your hard drive crashes you can never recover all the music you paid for at the iTunes store.</ul>I opted not to correct any of his statements because he was defining his entire opposition to Apple on a combination of bad information and a loudly proclaimed loyalty to Microsoft.  True Believers aren't worth talking to because they are unwilling to incorporate new or corrected information into their world view.  I've talked to guys like this before and when I say something like "Actually, the iPod will play any MP3 file.  Whether you get it from iTunes, Amazon, the web, or rip it from your CDs yourself" they go on a rant about Apple's DRM which doesn't allow AAC files to play on MP3 players other than the iPod.  Note that AAC is not MP3.  You correct their facts and they simply ignore what you say and respond to a different argument.  I vacilate as to whether their tactics are due to a fundamental mendacity or simply being too stupid to understand that AAC is not the same file format as MP3.  <p />Given the plethora of devices that allow you to stream iTunes to your tv or stereo, including Linksys, Slingbox, Wii, and *Microsoft XBOX*  it's just plain ignorance on the part of .NetGuy to be unaware.  Unless he was being intentionally dishonest.  Given my further experiences with him throughout the day I consider it a distinct possibility.<p />And I'll note that the linux zealots (I love linux, I use linux, but I'm not a fanatic about it) are worse than the Microsoft zealots.
