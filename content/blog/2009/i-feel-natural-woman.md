I am not one normally to adhere to gender stereotypes.  And who among us does not appreciate a good shampoo.  I somehow got signed up for a <a href="http://www.suave.com/nomics/index.html">Suavenomics</a> and they sent me a coupon for free product.  I like free, I wash my hair, so this seems just fine to me.  But I'm a bit confused at the letter they sent addressed to Matt in which they say <blockquote>We believe beauty should be recession proof, so we're giving <b>women like you</b> across the country a free bottle of Suave.</blockquote>Emphasis mine.  Sure, I'm not the most alpha of men, but how would they know that?  Because I realize this is all automated and I know a thing or three about programming I have to wonder in what error checking routine does the name "Matt" map to the adjective "woman"?<br /><br />But I'm not mad about it, I'll still take the free bottle.<br /><br />
