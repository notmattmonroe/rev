One of the top stories the past two days has been the <a href="http://www.cnn.com/2006/US/01/10/mouse.fire.ap/index.html">mouse that burned down the house</a>.  I didn't believe it when I first read it.  I instantly said to myself &quot;insurance scam&quot;.  The day after it hit the news the man said that wind blew burning leaves into the house (an event that would not be covered by most homeowners insurance as a result of negligence).  Then rapidly changed back to the original story, which would at least possibly be covered.  As a devout skeptic, I have unending faith in man's desire to rip off his fellow man.
