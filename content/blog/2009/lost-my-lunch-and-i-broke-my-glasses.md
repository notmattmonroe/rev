Title: Lost my lunch and I broke my glasses
Date: 2009-12-16 16:23
Slug: lost-my-lunch-and-i-broke-my-glasses
Category: personal
Status: published

In our kitchen is a school lunch calendar so that we know what is purportedly* going to be served each day.  Part of my job as Dad is to make lunch for Boo on days when the lunch option on offer is not one she wants.  She brings her lunch about 50% of the time.  Last night as I cleaned the kitchen and glanced at the calendar I noticed that for Tuesday it said in large green Sharpie "BRING".  My short term memory is not what it once was, but I was fairly sure I hadn't made lunch for her that morning.  So I went in the living room and asked what she ate for lunch.  "A cheeseburger.  There wasn't any lunch in my backpack."  This was accompanied by possibly the most accusatory look she's ever given me.  And she practices those looks.  Seriously.  I've caught her practicing various disapproving expressions in the mirror.  I apologized profusely of course and  said "Well, if I forget again tomorrow you're having Sloppy Joe for lunch."  She hates Sloppy Joe's so she said "YOU BETTER NOT FORGET MY LUNCH TOMORROW!!!!"<br /><br />This morning the very first thing she did was check her backpack to make sure I'd packed her lunch.  Which I had.  I even gave her an extra cookie.  Then oddly enough she claimed that she'd had mini hot dogs for lunch yesterday rather than the previously stated cheeseburger.  I'm very confused.<br /><br />*The specific accuracy of the calendars predictions is marginally greater than that of the weather forecast.
