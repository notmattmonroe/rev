On Bill Maher's excellent HBO show Real Time he repeated the popular (particularly in the business world) idea that in Chinese the word for crisis and opportunity are the same.  Given his views on medicine and vaccines I suppose I shouldn't be surprised that he doesn't recognize total horse crap when he sees it.  The best debunking of this nonsense comes from the esteemed <a href="http://www.pinyin.info/chinese/crisis.html">Victor H. Mair</a>.  Please, go read it.  And never repeat the woefully uninformed trope.  Note that saying such a thing in front of a native Chinese speaker will probably cause them to roll their eyes and laugh at you.
