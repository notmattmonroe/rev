There are many tools around these days to help the busy person remain organized, taken to the OCD extreme with the GTD movement to which I've aspired and failed.  My downfall is the reminders.  They pop up to let me know it's time to do something and I have a remarkable capacity to ignore these pop ups.  I have scheduled all sorts of routine maintenance tasks as reminders in iCal as well as various important events and projects.  A notification pops up and herein lies the real problem:  I hate being interrupted when I'm doing something.<br /><br />I have always hated distractions when I'm trying to focus.   Having kids means that's pretty much a constant.  So I'm trying to learn to adapt to an interrupt driven life.  Step one is I block out time for tasks after the kids go to bed.  That eliminates the major class of interrupts though it does constrain those tasks to being things that can be done after dark.  It's a start.  I'm not sure what step two is...
