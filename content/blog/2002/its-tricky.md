"You have a hierarchy of shit, and you're not sure what shit WebSphere expects."<p/>In our <a href="http://www.punitiveart.com/blog/noMoreWords/blogentry_view">fozzle</a> meeting yesterday, I proposed an approach to determining if a particular widget should display or not.  I'm not a JSP coder, I've done minimal work with it and don't know a whole lot about the technology.  The developer looked at me aghast, and informed me that it would be a huge ugly hack to get it to work.  This is a developer whose technical skills I respect, so I am quite sure she is correct. Oddly, I did a proof of concept of it in Zope in about 2 minutes.
