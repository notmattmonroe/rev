After Hurricane Katrina our agency issued Emergency Survival kits to all employees and contractors (nice that they actually included the contractors, as we normally rank somewhere below carpet mites).  Except because they were worth $50 each they had to stay locked in a manager's office.  Because in the event of an emergency I'm sure we'll all have access to the manager's office.  <br /><br />Today, our manager began unceremoniously dropping them on people's desks and telling us they had to go in our overhead cabinets.  Being the curious sort I decided to see what was in it.  <ul><li>a flashlight</li><li>two D batteries for flashlight </li><li>a 2400 calorie nutrient bar</li><li>emergency survival blanket</li><li>first aid kit</li><li>6 125ml pouches of emergency water</li></ul>And all in a calming red carrier that says "SURVIVAL KIT" on the front.  One of the water pouches in my kit leaked.
