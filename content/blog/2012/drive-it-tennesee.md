Title: Drive it to Tennesee
Date: 2012-01-24 17:12
Slug: drive-it-tennesee
Category: personal
Status: published

A while back my old mentor from Colorado stopped by on a cross country trip.  We were talking about The Good Old Days(tm) and he reminded me of the software company that hired our group to be their research lab.  We were all working for a company in Boulder at the time and had previously worked together for another company.  We knew each other pretty well, we'd been working closely for 2-3 years at that point and this was our newest adventure together.  <p />For some reason the new company felt it was important for us to all fly to the coast for a week of Team Building exercises.  We were hired as a team.  We designed vanity license plates for ourselves, we played musical chairs.  I'm not kidding.  They spent upwards of $10K on hotels, flights, cars, food, etc to teach us to work together as a team through stupid activities that would embarrass any improv group.  <p />This perhaps should have been a warning flag for us that they didn't necessarily consider the most effective use of their available funds.  Which sadly became very clear a year later when they announced the research group was being shut down as they simply couldn't afford us.  I don't believe they ever implemented any of the work product we came up with.  We had a number of great ideas on how to improve their products and services, but they were very set in their existing paradigm.<p />One of the most amusing points of the trip was that I was nominated to be the group driver as I'd lived in L.A. and the others were nervous about dealing with the insanity that is coast traffic.  At one point I U-turned over the median at a toll booth because the booth operator told us we were headed the wrong way to get to our destination.  This is how you get places.
