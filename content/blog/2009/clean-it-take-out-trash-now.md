Title: Clean it up, take out the trash now
Date: 2009-04-06 15:33
Slug: clean-it-take-out-trash-now
Category: personal
Status: published

On the importance of maintaining your tools.  I was whipping together a quick little project for better organizing some electronics and was getting frustrated by my inability to get a straight cut on the table saw.  Double and triple checked square on the mitre and finally came to the determination that a) I need a better mitre and 2) the blade needs to be changed.  I dealt with the last item first because it's easier and desperately needed.  I began to think back to when I last changed it.   Hmmm.  That would be never.  So I've been running the saw for 8 years without changing the blade or cleaning out the housing.  <br /><br />Whenever I get time to work in the garage, I jump into a project because I don't get enough time to do everything I want so I bypass general cleaning and tool maintenance.   My poor shop vac got one hell of a workout.  I managed to fill up half of a thirteen gallon trash can with the sawdust from the saw housing.  New blade installed, still to be adjusted for true to the fence and mitre, but that's progress and the whole setup looks almost new.<br /><br />My workbench, on the other hand, is a total disaster.<br /><img src="/media/postimgs/IMG_0111.jpg" alt="messy workbench" width="300" height="400" />
