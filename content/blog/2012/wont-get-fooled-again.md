Title: Won't Get Fooled Again
Date: 2012-12-13 00:58
Slug: wont-get-fooled-again
Category: personal
Status: published

It would appear the QR Code scams are on the rise.  If you aren't familiar with QR Codes, they're those odd little squares with geometric patterns in them you see on ads etc.  <p /><img src="http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9b/Wikipedia_mobile_en.svg" /><p />Scan this little box with your smart phone and it will take you to a web page or give commands to your phone to do other things.  Like send text messages, activate your camera and start streaming it to the internet without your knowledge, etc etc.<p />When I first heard of these many years back my first thought was "Wow, that's an incredibly high risk thing to do with your phone, why would anyone even consider it?"    I appear to be in the minority in that view.  Then again I also wouldn't reveal my password in <a href="http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/technology/3639679.stm">exchange for a candy bar</a>.
