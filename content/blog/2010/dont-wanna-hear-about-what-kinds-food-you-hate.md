Title: Don't wanna hear about what kinds of food you hate
Date: 2010-02-17 16:41
Slug: dont-wanna-hear-about-what-kinds-food-you-hate
Category: personal
Status: published

This weekend I got truly fed up with the lack of dinner eating going on.  Dinner is always the worst meal of the day (and one of only two I have to deal with on weekdays, so maybe lunch is even worse but my lovely wife hasn't complained of it).  I've tried threatening, bribing, bargaining, I've taken away tv privileges, banned after dinner snacks, all to little long term avail. <br /><br />So Sunday night as the kids were not eating their dinner I got out the Valentines cupcakes from McArthur's bakery and set them in the center of the table.  No dinner?  No dessert.  Sure we've tried that approach in the past, but some abstract 'dessert' that's out in the kitchen is far less compelling than a tangible one sitting mere inches from you.  Amazing how the delay tactics, dawdling, playing with food, etc stopped cold.  I'm not above bribing my kids.
