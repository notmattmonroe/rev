At the behest of <a href="http://wordtoyourmutha.blogspot.com/2007/11/mutha-issues-challenge.html">Mutha</a>:<br /><br />As a result of our adoption adventure I've become very interested in China, particularly views of it from expatriates as I continue to dream about getting a job in Hong Kong.  I follow about a dozen China blogs including a few group blogs, and one my favorites is <a href="http://washingtonbureau.typepad.com/china/">China Rises</a>.  Tim is a newspaper Bureau Chief located in Beijing and has been in China for many years.  His perspective is a nice counterpoint to both the official Chinese media stances and the western media/government positions on issues of the day.
