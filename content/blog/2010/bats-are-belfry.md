To the Bat Desk!  My eldest is goth.  She is also 6.  Go figure.  When I built a desk for her middle sister and mentioned I was going to paint it pink, Eldest whined that she wanted a BLACK desk WITH BATS.  That was her precise emphasis.  So I decided we would paint the desks as a family.  Each child got to paint their own desk while I supervised.  It was of course a disaster and after they went to bed I went out and touched up what I could to minimize it.
