In the fall I stopped shaving.  Several people commented on mydecision to grow a beard.  Let me be clear on this.  The decision wasnot 'to grow a beard'. The beard was a side effect of the real point:to not have to shave.  It is true that the one naturally follows fromthe other, but that does not mean that the most obvious result wasthe primary intent.  Something to consider when trying to divine themotives of others in many situations.  That's good advice there,son.  Don't forget it.  As I always do.<br /><br />But I digress.   It only took about two weeks before the beard lookedgood, or as good as it was ever going to at least.  It was I believeanother two weeks before my wife noticed.  This past weekend thetopic came up again and I was advised by both her and Boo that Ishould shave.  So I did.  To her credit it only took two days for mylovely bride to notice.  Boo noticed right away and gave adisapproving look when she said "You didn't shave it ALL."  Which Iwon't.
