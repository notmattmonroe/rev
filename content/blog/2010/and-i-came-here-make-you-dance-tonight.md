Title: And I came here to make you dance tonight
Date: 2010-04-06 14:22
Slug: and-i-came-here-make-you-dance-tonight
Category: personal
Status: published

There is a concept particularly popular in the world of music, though I know it exists in many other areas of interest, that I simply do not accede to.  Guilty pleasures.  That song, that band, that video, even that genre that you are embarrased for people to see in your collection or to hear you listening to or singing along with.  Isn't the whole point of music enjoyment?  Why would you be mortified for people to find out what brings you joy?  Is our society so Calvinist that only the 'right' things should bring us joy and aberration is to be hidden away with some sort of deep shame and guilt?  Is it the height of social transgression to acknowledge that RATT still brings a smile to my face when I'm in the right mood?  Is there an abiding sense of wrong that must accompany the pop stylings of the Black Eyed Peas?  At long last is it really necessary to beat up on Billy Joel or Barenaked Ladies simply because they are popular and thus somehow unworthy of admiration in any but an ironic sense?<br /><br />I blame the hipsters.<br /><br />In all things be they music, movies, food, sex, books, art, enjoyment is the true purpose.   Enjoy what you enjoy.  Embrace it, be true to your own self, don't hide it, don't deny it.
