s been some time since Seal has graced us with new music. On IV solidly declares that he is the heir to the throne of R&B. In the spirit of greats like Teddy Pendergrass and Marvin Gaye, Seal delivers with a smoothness and passion that make you want to get up and dance. Whether shaking it to upbeat tracks like "Get It Together" and "Let Me Roll" or slow dancing with your true love to "Don't Make Me Wait" and "Heavenly", this is not an album for sitting down.<BR><BR>Hot Spots: Get It Together, Let Me Roll, Heavenly, Love's Divine, Waiting For You
