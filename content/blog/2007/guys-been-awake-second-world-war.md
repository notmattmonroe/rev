Henry Rollins once said &quot;Sleep is for lightweights&quot;. He was, of course, joking. Much to my amusement, the quote at the header of the page while I'm writing this is &quot;Better living through denial&quot;.<br /><br />Here has been an unfortunately fairly typical 2-3 times a week nightly routine of babies waking up and needing to be soothed back to sleep:<br /><br />baby 1: 9.15-9.30<br />baby 1: 10.20-10.55<br />baby 2: 10.40-11.15<br />baby 2: 11:30-11.50<br />baby 2: 12.05-12.30<br />baby 2: 2.20-2.50<br />baby 2: 3.30-3.50<br />baby1: 4.05-4.20<br />baby 2: 4.30-4.45<br />baby 2: up at 5.10<br />baby 1: up at 5.45<br /><br />They aren't teething ( or at least Orajel hasn't helped if they are, and I don't feel any teeth making progress), they've both been to the pediatrician and don't have ear infections or anything like that. It's not gas, it's not colic (because they can be relatively easily soothed back to sleep).<br /><br />And before any of you anti-vaccine zealots start frothing at the mouth and blaming the flu vaccine (or whatever medical success you're fomenting against these days) realize that this started happening before their shots and recurs intermittently.<br />Yes, I know &quot;But they're Indigo Children and they KNEW they'd be getting evil vaccines and were acting out to protest in the only way they know how.&quot; I prefer to live in the real world, not a bad sci-fi novel, so I'll continue to operate from a reality-based set of assumptions, thanks so much.<br /><br />
