I don't recall where I read this, but there was a blog entry a few days ago from a woman criticising the iTunes Music Store.  Her position was that since she wasn't that into music and didn't care much about buying songs or albums, then the iTunes Music Store was doomed to failure unless they changed their business model to cater to people like her.<br /><br />This would be like me telling Tommy Hilfiger that unless they catered to my fashion sense (and I do have some.  Stop laughing.) then they would go bankrupt.  <br /><br />If you aren't part of the target market for a product or service, chances are the company is aware of the segment you are a part of but doesn't view it as one they are interested in.  They're just not that into you.  Move on.<br />
