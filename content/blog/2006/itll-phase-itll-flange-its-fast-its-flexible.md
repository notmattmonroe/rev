A few bands and albums I'm currently enamored with and may someday get around to writing reviews of:<br /><br /><a href="http://www.gramrabbit.com/">Gram Rabbit</a><br />Very quirky and slightly country tinged (not crossover/redneck woman crap, real classic country) alt-rock. The singer is vaguely reminiscent of Gwen Stefani, but less affected.<br /><a href="http://www.toolband.com/">Tool</a> - 10,000 Days. It's Tool. Heavy and kickass<br /><a href="http://www.snowpatrol.net/">Snow Patrol</a> - Eyes Open. I don't like it as much as the last album, but it's growing on me. It seems like every other song is great.<br /><a href="http://www.satriani.com/">Joe Satriani</a> - Super Colossal. This is his first album that's interested me in some time. The podcasts of 'the making of' probably helped spark that interest.<br /><a href="http://www.minus5.com/">Minus 5</a> - It bothers me that I'm finding bands classified as alt-country appealing. I like to think of them as Wilcoish.<br /><a href="http://www.wannabattle.com/flowfessionals.php">Flowfessionals compilation</a> - It's got flow. I'm down with that.<br /><a href="http://www.kiramusic.com/">Kira and the Kindred Spirits</a> - Fun Danish garage rock, though she has more than a little torch singer in her.<br /><a href="http://profile.myspace.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=user.viewprofile&friendid=56975988">Jahi</a> - Serious MC with intelligent lyrics and great flow. Stress is the cousin of death.<br /> <a href="http://www.arcticmonkeys.com/">Arctic Monkeys</a> - UK rock with no pretense of depth. A rock version of The Streets.<br /> <a href="http://wearewolves.net/">We Are Wolves</a> - Crazy Canadians. Hard rocking screaming electronica.<br /> <br />Also, the Indiefeed podcast has introduced me to about a dozen other artists I want to hear more of:<br /><a href="http://www.brainfailure.com/">Brain Failure</a> - Chinese punk rock <br /><a href="http://www.arkitektmusic.com/">Arkitekt</a> - just plain old rock <br /><a href="http://www.taxidoll.com/">Taxi Doll</a> - ambience heavy pop<br /><a href="http://www.theevilqueens.com/">The Evil Queens</a> - Big heavy rock<br /><a href="http://www.garageband.com/artist/komputadora">Komputadora</a> - Weird electronic/rap<br /><span style="text-decoration: underline;"></span> <a href="http://www.phenomenauts.com/">Phenomenauts</a> - Fun electro-rockabilly <br /><span style="text-decoration: underline;"></span> <a href="http://www.blingkong.com/">Bling Kong</a> - Cheerleaders. Really. Clearly started as a joke, and might still be. But it's a funny one <br /><span style="text-decoration: underline;"></span> <a href="http://www.stonecuttersmusic.com/">Mad Marge and the Stonecutters</a> - Rockabilly with a vicious edge.<br /><a href="http://www.ahabrex.com/">Ahab Rex</a> - Glam era Bowie meets Trent Reznor<br /><a href="http://www.asobiseksu.com/">Asobi Seksu</a> - Interesting mix of Japanese and English lyrics, music mixes 50's pop and 90's grunge.<br /><a href="http://www.slowride.net/" /><a href="http://www.mountaincon.com/">Mountain Con</a><span style="text-decoration: underline;"></span> - Kinda like Franz Ferdinand, but interesting.<br /><a href="http://www.thepinkertones.com/">Pinker Tones - </a>Devo meets Super Mario Brothers with a latin flavor.<br />Lesbians on Ecstasy (I will not google them from work) - Very cool electro-rock. <br /><br /><br />Very very disappointing: RHCP - Stadium Arcadium. Of course, I haven't liked much that they've done since Mother's Milk.<br /><br />
