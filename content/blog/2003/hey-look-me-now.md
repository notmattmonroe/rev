Title: Hey, look at me now
Date: 2003-03-26 21:08
Slug: hey-look-me-now
Category: personal
Status: published

New reviews are up.  I've tried several times to review Good Charlotte, but can't bring myself to listen to the whole CD.  It's very predictable and boring.  The new Hootie is good, but not really my kind of thing.  Same with The White Stripes.  I just don't get the appeal of them.  Oh well.  Taproot: cliche.  Jack Johnson: wishes he was Jimmy Buffet. Liam Lynch rocks, I need more.  System of a Down is increasingly silly.  They're almost a self parody at this point.  If they get any more ridiculous they'll have to change their name to Mudvayne.  I'm fairly sure Glassjaw is the sort of band people mean when they refer to emo.  I'm unimpressed.  Whiny and irritating.  Same for Saliva.  Simple Plan is marginally better (they're not nearly as whiny, and they almost rock).  Raveonettes: psychedelic garage rock.  If you're into it, it's not bad.  Revis is fairly generic rock/hard rock.  Decent voice, not really suited to the material.  Favorite is sort of emo with attitude.   Not bad, has potential.  BoySetsFire is a modern day Slayer.  They probably say Heavy Fucking Metal.  Last but not least, we have Unloco.  Half this damn sampler is emo bullshit.  It's better than country (what's not?) but nothing I would choose to listen to. <p>reviewed<ul><li><a href="http://revmatt.com/reviews/2003/mar/26/live-brazil/">Concrete Blonde - Live In Brazil</li><li><a href="http://revmatt.com/reviews/2003/mar/26/meteora/">Linkin Park - Meteora</li><li><a href="http://revmatt.com/reviews/2003/mar/26/animositisomina/">Ministry - Animositisomina</li><li><a href="http://revmatt.com/reviews/2003/mar/26/faceless/">Godsmack - Faceless</li><li><a href="http://revmatt.com/reviews/2003/mar/26/causing-catastrophe/">Flickerstick - Causing A Catastrophe</li></ul>
