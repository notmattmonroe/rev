Barenaked Ladies are well known for the extensive improvisation during their shows.  Not esoteric jazz style long solo improvisations.  Switching up lyrics to reference current events or inside jokes,  doing stream of consciousness rhyming couplets made up on the spot, and trying to outwit each other in the process.<br /><br />I'm listening to the iTunes Originals album and in Pinch me he throws in the line <br /><br /><div style="text-align: center;">put the sprinkler on the lawn <br />and run through with my iPod on<br />take a drink right from the hose<br />then change into my sisters' clothes<br /></div><br />They do this sort of thing live all the time.  Just changing one word can completely alter the tone of a song.  &quot;I mix up my grandma&quot; evokes a whole different idea than &quot;I mix up my grammar&quot;.  I also thought it was cool that one of my favorite lines of their entire catalog is also one of their favorites:<br /><br />You try to scream but it only comes out as a yawn<br /><br />
