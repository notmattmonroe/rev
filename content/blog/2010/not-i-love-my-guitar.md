So with the move et all things have been just a tad chaotic.  I've cobbled together a makeshift workshop in a corner of the basement until I can get around to doing it right.  <br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/new_workshop.jpg" alt=" New workshop" width="400" height="536" /><br /><br />I cut a few pieces to address a pressing need in the office.  At the old place I hung all the guitars on the wall.  Not so much an option here, and single guitar stands take up a lot of room.  I know I had a three-way guitar stand at one point but I apparently got rid of it at some point.  Dammit.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/stand_pieces.jpg" alt=" Pieces for stand" width="400" height="299" /><br /><br />Two base pieces clamped for cutting to ensure everything fits all the way across.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/clamped.jpg" alt=" clamped together" width="400" height="536" /><br /><br />The base has come together nicely.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/base_done.jpg" alt=" base finished and glue drying" width="400" height="536" /><br /><br />Pegs in place and drying.  Slightly sloppy work there, as I measured and marked the edge points but then drilled as though those were the center points, so I had to angle the pegs in to allow the necks to rest properly.  Ah well.  As with most of my prototypes this one will likely never be replaced with a "real" one.<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/pegs.jpg" alt=" Pegs in place" width="400" height="536" /><br /><br />The final product in place.  This took a total of a half hour Wednesday night and a half hour Thursday night.  Had I spent more time it would be a bit more polished, but it will do for now.  It accomplishes the core goal of getting all the guitars in a stable safe-ish place (as safe as possible with kids around) and takes up way less room than individual stands would.	<br /><br /><img src="/media/revmatt/gstand/done.jpg" alt=" New workshop" width="400" height="536" />
