There are times when I think the Greater Internet Dickwad Theory is not entirely accurate.  I don't think the anonymity is required.  When considering why someone is behaving in a way that I do not expect or necessarily understand I try to be a generous person.  I try to give the benefit of the doubt as much as I can.  But then I think "Hmmm, if I were a more cynical person..." and consider the motivations that I consider to be most likely close to accurate.  These motivations are rarely flattering to the person in question, but in  my experience even then I often err on the side of generosity.  Because people suck and should be destroyed.
