Foo Fighters rose from the ashes of Nirvana in the early 90's. It was widely predicted they would fail miserably, as Kurt was obviously the musical genius. The subsequent years, albums, and string of hits tends to bring that opinion into question. Dave and friends have shown a remarkable ability to write catchy yet aggressive songs, and their quirky videos certainly don't hurt. This is music for the cheerfuly angry. It's about relationships, confusion, frustration, the makings of great music.<BR><BR>Hot Spots: Overdrive, All My Life, Times Like These, Tired of You.
