The Boo's desk is now complete.  It measure 24 inches square and has a slide out keyboard tray.  I used a nice sheet of 'cabinet grade' (e.g.: $40/sheet) plywood which is smooth and has a nice color to it.  The longest part of the process was waiting for the finish to cure.  I cut four panels from the sheet (two sides, one back, one work surface), routed a dado for the work surface and to connect the back to the sides.  The edges were all rounded to make them more kid friendly.
